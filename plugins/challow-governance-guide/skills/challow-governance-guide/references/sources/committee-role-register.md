## Safeguarding conflict arrangement

Louise is the Club's Safeguarding Officer. Where Louise has an actual or potential conflict of interest, is the subject of a concern or is otherwise unable to act independently, Narelle, as Resolution Lead, is the alternative Club point of contact. If both are conflicted, the matter should be referred to the appropriate England Netball safeguarding contact or other competent authority.

## Updating the register

Operational and required governance role assignments may be updated by Management Committee resolution and recorded in the minutes, subject to the Constitution. Core elected Officer roles are normally elected at the AGM.
