# Communications, Events & Sponsorship Officer

Operational role. Current role holder: Lynsey and Daisy. Typical commitment: 2–4 hrs/month normally; 5–10 hrs/month around events or campaigns.

## Current role holder

Lynsey and Daisy

## Role category

Operational role

## Typical time commitment

2–4 hrs/month normally; 5–10 hrs/month around events or campaigns

## Key responsibilities

• Coordinate Club communications.
• Maintain website/social media content.
• Plan and coordinate Club social and fundraising events.
• Develop and maintain sponsor relationships.
• Coordinate sensitive communications with the relevant governance leads.
• Maintain a simple communications and events calendar.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
