# Policy Register

The index of local governance documents and England Netball policies that Challow adopts or follows.

## Club governance documents

Constitution; Committee & Role Register; RACI Responsibility Matrix; Individual Role Descriptions; Financial Management & Delegated Authority Policy; Data Protection & Privacy Policy; Complaints & Resolution Policy; Conflict of Interest Policy & Register.

## England Netball policies / guidance

The Club should follow the current England Netball safeguarding policies and reporting routes, Codes of Conduct and disciplinary framework, Gender Eligibility & Participation policy, membership and registration requirements, and applicable insurance / recognised activity requirements.

## Operational policies to add when needed

Health, Safety & Risk Policy; Social Media & Communications Policy; Volunteer & Coach Management / onboarding procedure; Equipment & Facilities procedure; Team Selection / Playing Policy; Photography / media consent guidance.

The governance portal must not be used as a safeguarding case-management system. The Club uses current England Netball safeguarding tools and reporting routes and records only neutral case reference numbers in routine governance records.

## Policy control

The Secretary should keep the current approved version, approval date and review date for each Club policy. Where a national England Netball policy is referenced, the Club should follow the current published version rather than retain an obsolete local copy.
