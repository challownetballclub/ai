# Data Protection & Privacy Policy

How the club handles personal information, privacy, access, retention and data incidents.

## Purpose

This policy explains the Club's approach to handling personal information and should be read with the Club's member privacy notice and applicable England Netball privacy information.

## Core principles

Use personal information lawfully, fairly and transparently; collect only what is reasonably needed; keep it accurate; retain it only as long as necessary; restrict access to those who need it; and apply additional care to sensitive information such as safeguarding and health information.

## Typical Club information

Member and parent/carer contact details; membership and registration data; emergency or medical information where needed; coach and volunteer information; payment records; photographs or video where applicable; and safeguarding, welfare or complaint information where necessary.

## Responsibilities

The Data Protection Lead coordinates privacy compliance and data incidents. The Registrar maintains membership data. The Secretary maintains governance records. The Treasurer protects financial records. All Committee members and volunteers must use personal information only for authorised Club purposes.

## Sharing information

Personal information may be shared with England Netball, leagues, competition organisers, venues, service providers or other bodies where there is a lawful and legitimate reason. Members should be informed about material sharing through an appropriate privacy notice.

## Website contact and device storage

The website contact form collects the sender's name, email address, intended Club role and message so the Club can receive and reply to the enquiry. Unless the selected role is configured for confidential delivery, validated messages are kept in the Club's private Azure storage for up to 365 days and are available to authenticated Hub editors. For confidential roles, including safeguarding and resolution by default, the email is delivered to the allocated role address without retaining the message or delivery record in the website archive.

If a visitor explicitly selects **Remember my name and email on this device**, only those two fields are stored in that browser for up to 180 days to prefill later contact forms. Message text and selected roles are not stored on the device. The visitor can remove the saved details at any time from the contact form or through browser storage controls.

## Access, storage and retention

Use role-based access where available, avoid unnecessary storage in personal accounts or devices, use secure passwords, review access when roles change, and review retained information at least annually.

## Governance portal restrictions

The governance portal is for routine club administration, agendas, approved minutes, decisions, actions, risks, opportunities and non-personal supporting documents. Committee members must not upload personal, medical, safeguarding, disciplinary, complaint or case-management information. Safeguarding records must remain in the current England Netball safeguarding tools and processes. The portal may record only a neutral reference number and procedural status, without names, allegations, health information or other case details.

Access to private governance records is limited to current Committee members on the maintained committee access list. Access must be removed promptly when a person leaves the Committee and reviewed at least annually. Public minutes must be separately reviewed and redacted before publication.

## Incidents and rights requests

Loss, unauthorised access, accidental disclosure or other suspected personal-data incidents should be reported promptly to the Data Protection Lead. Requests about access, correction, deletion or other data rights should also be referred promptly.
