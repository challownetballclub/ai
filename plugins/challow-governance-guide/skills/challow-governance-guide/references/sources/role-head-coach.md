# Head Coach

Operational role. Current role holder: Louise. Typical commitment: 2–4 hrs/month governance/coordination, excluding personal team coaching.

## Current role holder

Louise

## Role category

Operational role

## Typical time commitment

2–4 hrs/month governance/coordination, excluding personal team coaching

## Key responsibilities

• Set and coordinate the Club's coaching approach and standards.
• Support coaches with planning and development.
• Coordinate player-development principles.
• Work with Safeguarding to promote safe practice.
• Identify coaching capacity and development needs.
• Provide coaching updates to the Committee.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
