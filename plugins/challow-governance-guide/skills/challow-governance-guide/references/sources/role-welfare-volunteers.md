# Welfare & Volunteer Coordinator

Operational role. Current role holder: Narelle. Typical commitment: 1–3 hrs/month; higher at season start and events.

## Current role holder

Narelle

## Role category

Operational role

## Typical time commitment

1–3 hrs/month; higher at season start and events

## Key responsibilities

• Promote a positive member and volunteer experience.
• Recruit and coordinate parent and other volunteers.
• Maintain a view of volunteer gaps and support allocation.
• Signpost welfare concerns to the correct Safeguarding or Resolution route.
• Support volunteer onboarding and recognition.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
