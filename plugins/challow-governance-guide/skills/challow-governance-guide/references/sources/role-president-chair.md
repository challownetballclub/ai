# President / Chairperson

Core elected Officer. Current role holder: Louise. Typical commitment: 2–4 hrs/month normally; 4–8 hrs around AGM, major issues or planning.

## Current role holder

Louise

## Role category

Core elected Officer

## Typical time commitment

2–4 hrs/month normally; 4–8 hrs around AGM, major issues or planning

## Key responsibilities

• Provide leadership and direction for the Club and ensure the Committee operates effectively.
• Chair Committee meetings, the AGM and General Meetings.
• Ensure decisions, actions and governance obligations are followed through.
• Act as a senior representative of the Club.
• Support other Committee members and help resolve gaps in ownership or delivery.
• Declare and manage conflicts appropriately.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
