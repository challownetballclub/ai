# Treasurer

Core elected Officer. Current role holder: Helen. Typical commitment: 2–4 hrs/month; 5–8 hrs at budgeting/year-end.

## Current role holder

Helen

## Role category

Core elected Officer

## Typical time commitment

2–4 hrs/month; 5–8 hrs at budgeting/year-end

## Key responsibilities

• Maintain accurate financial records and oversight of Club bank accounts.
• Process or oversee authorised income, payments and reimbursements.
• Prepare regular financial reports and annual member information.
• Prepare and monitor the Club budget.
• Maintain appropriate financial controls and records.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
