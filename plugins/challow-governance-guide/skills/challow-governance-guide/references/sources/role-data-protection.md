# Data Protection Lead

Required governance role. Current role holder: Louise. Typical commitment: 0.5–1.5 hrs/month; additional time for incidents or major changes.

## Current role holder

Louise

## Role category

Required governance role

## Typical time commitment

0.5–1.5 hrs/month; additional time for incidents or major changes

## Key responsibilities

• Coordinate the Club's data protection and privacy responsibilities.
• Maintain or coordinate privacy notices and appropriate access controls.
• Help ensure information is collected, stored, shared and deleted appropriately.
• Coordinate responses to data incidents and rights requests.
• Work with the Secretary, Registrar and Communications leads.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
