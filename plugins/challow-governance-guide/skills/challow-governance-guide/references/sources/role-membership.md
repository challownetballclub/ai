# Registrar & Membership Officer

Operational role. Current role holder: Katie. Typical commitment: 1–2 hrs/month in season; 6–12 hrs during annual registration/renewal.

## Current role holder

Katie

## Role category

Operational role

## Typical time commitment

1–2 hrs/month in season; 6–12 hrs during annual registration/renewal

## Key responsibilities

• Maintain membership and player registration records.
• Coordinate annual renewals and affiliations.
• Support team eligibility and registration requirements.
• Work with the Data Protection Lead on member-data handling.
• Provide membership reporting to the Committee.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
