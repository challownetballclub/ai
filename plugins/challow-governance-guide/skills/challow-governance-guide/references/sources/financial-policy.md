# Financial Management & Delegated Authority Policy

Spending limits, budget authority, banking controls, reimbursements, financial reporting and escalation of financial concerns.

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Challow Netball Club authorises expenditure, controls payments, manages budgets and reports its financial position. It sits beneath the Constitution and may be amended by Committee resolution.

## Principles

Club money must be used only for legitimate Club purposes. Approval to incur expenditure and authority to release a bank payment are separate controls. Nobody may approve a payment, reimbursement or financial benefit to themselves. Conflicts must be declared. Supporting receipts or invoices should be retained where practical. Financial oversight is a collective Committee responsibility.

## Delegated spending authority

Up to £100 — relevant role holder, within an approved budget and their area of responsibility.
£101–£500 — Treasurer plus one other core Officer, within approved budget.
£501–£1,500 — Management Committee approval by simple majority, recorded in minutes.
Over £1,500 — specific Management Committee approval recorded before commitment.
Any unbudgeted spend over £250 — Management Committee approval.
Contracts longer than 12 months — Management Committee approval.
Payments or benefits to a Committee member or connected person — Management Committee approval excluding the conflicted person.
Emergency safety or safeguarding expenditure — President / Chair or Treasurer may authorise where delay creates material risk, with prompt reporting to Committee.

## Banking and payment controls

The Club should maintain a bank account in its own name. Where supported, payments should use two-person authorisation. Normal bank approvers should include the Treasurer plus another authorised Officer. A person must not be the sole approver of a payment to themselves. Bank access should be removed promptly when an authorised person leaves their role.

## Budget and reporting

The Treasurer must prepare and circulate the proposed annual budget to the Management Committee in July for the forthcoming season. The Management Committee must consider and approve the annual budget in August, with the approval recorded in the minutes. At regular Committee meetings the Treasurer should report bank balance, income and expenditure, material commitments, budget position and significant financial risks. Annual financial information should be presented to members at the AGM.

## Purchasing and reimbursement

Value for money should be considered for material purchases. Multiple quotations should normally be sought for significant or unusual purchases where practical. Personal expenditure on behalf of the Club should be pre-authorised where practical and reimbursed against reasonable evidence.

## Fraud, error and financial concerns

Suspected fraud, theft, serious irregularity or misuse of Club funds must be reported immediately to the President / Chair and Treasurer unless either is implicated, in which case it must be escalated to an unconflicted Officer or Committee member and, where appropriate, the bank, insurer, England Netball or relevant authority.
