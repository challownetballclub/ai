# Challow Netball Hub: committee website how-to guide

This public guide explains routine use of the Challow Netball Hub. It describes workflows, not private Club records. Screen labels can change as the website improves.

## Sign in

1. Open the Netball Hub menu and choose Microsoft or passkey sign-in.
2. Returning passkey users should choose **Use your passkey**. This reuses the existing passkey; **Set up a passkey** is for a new trusted device or first-time enrolment.
3. On a personal device, **Keep me signed in on this device for 30 days** can remain selected. Untick it on a shared device.
4. After sign-in, choose **Admin**. Use **Sign out** when finished on a shared device.

The Microsoft route is retained as recovery. Passkey access depends on the member remaining active in the Committee access list.

## Understand the two work areas

- **Content** (`/admin.html`) publishes public-facing information: Posts, Registration, Teams and Training, Fundraising and Committee Roles.
- **Club Operations** (`/governance-admin.html`) manages committee work: meetings, decisions, actions, risks, opportunities, equipment, calendars, controlled documents, sponsors, activity and the Bin.

## Publish or update public content

### Posts

1. Open **Content → Posts**.
2. Create a new post or open an existing one.
3. Complete the title, summary, body, publication dates and any image or action link.
4. Save, then check the public Hub. Publication dates and the published flag control visibility.

### Registration

1. Open **Content → Registration**.
2. Set the status, public wording, dates, image and optional external action.
3. Update the **New to Challow** supporting section if required.
4. Save and verify the public registration page.

### Teams and Training

1. Open **Content → Teams & Training**.
2. Add or update each group, time, venue, age group and supporting information.
3. Save and verify the public timetable and group details.

### Committee Roles

1. Open **Content → Committee Roles**.
2. Update the public role holder and guidance separately from private notification settings.
3. Use **Do not retain messages sent to this role** for confidential delivery where appropriate.
4. Save and verify the public Committee and Contacts pages.

Notification addresses control message delivery only; they never grant website access.

## Run committee governance

### Schedule and run a meeting

1. Open **Club Operations → Schedule Meetings** and create the meeting, attendees and agenda.
2. At the meeting, open **Run Meeting**, confirm attendance and quorum, then record decisions and any new actions, risks or opportunities.
3. Save draft minutes. Do not include personal, medical, safeguarding, disciplinary or complaint case details.
4. At the following quorate meeting, record acceptance of the previous minutes and any agreed amendment.
5. Publish only an approved, appropriately redacted public version.

### Decisions, actions, risks and opportunities

- Use **Decisions** for a clear resolution, outcome and voting record.
- Use **Actions** for work with an owner, due date, progress and closure evidence.
- Use **Risks** for threats requiring ownership, controls and review.
- Use **Opportunities** for beneficial ideas with an owner and next step.
- Link records to the relevant meeting where possible so the audit trail explains why they exist.

### Controlled documents

1. Open **Club Operations → Documents**.
2. Choose the correct folder, then create or update the document metadata.
3. Upload a replacement as a new controlled version rather than silently overwriting governance history.
4. Set public or committee visibility deliberately. Only approved public documents appear on the public Hub.

### Equipment

1. Define equipment once in the shared catalogue, including stock and replacement duration.
2. Create groups or kit bags and allocate catalogue items without exceeding available stock.
3. Record purchases, replacements, warranty claims and repairs against the relevant equipment.
4. Public issue reporting uses the group's QR link plus its separately supplied reporting code. Submitted issues create a linked committee Action.

### Calendar and sponsors

- Use **Calendar** to maintain public events and obtain the public or private subscription link. Treat the private committee subscription URL as a committee-equivalent bearer credential.
- Use **Sponsors** to maintain approved logos, publication dates, websites and member offers. Confirm the Club has permission to publish each logo.

### Activity and Bin

- **Activity** is the append-only explanation of governance changes.
- Moving a record to the **Bin** is a recoverable soft deletion. Restore it from the Bin if it was removed in error.

## Manage committee access

Administrators can use **Club Operations → Members** to add or deactivate members, grant administrator access, review or revoke passkeys and sessions, sign a member out everywhere, and inspect sign-in activity. Removing a person from a public role does not automatically remove their sign-in access; update the Committee access list as a separate offboarding step.

## Information that must not be entered

Do not put personal safeguarding, medical, disciplinary, complaint or case-management details into Club Operations, AI questions or public content. Use the appropriate England Netball or confidential Club process. The website may hold only a neutral reference and procedural status where needed.

## If the guide does not answer the question

Ask the relevant role holder or an administrator. For governance questions, start from the [machine-readable governance source hub](https://www.challownetballclub.org.uk/ai/governance.md) and cite the controlling policy.
