# Challow Netball Club Constitution

The core rules for how Challow Netball Club is governed, including membership, officers, committee powers, meetings, finance, safeguarding and constitutional change.

## 1. Name

The name of the organisation shall be Challow Netball Club (the Club).

## 2. Purpose and Objectives

The Club exists to provide, develop and promote netball in a safe, inclusive, well-governed and enjoyable environment.

The Club will provide opportunities for members to participate in training, competition and recognised netball activity; promote player, coach, umpire and volunteer development; operate in accordance with England Netball and relevant affiliated bodies; promote equality, inclusion, fair play, safeguarding and welfare; and manage finances, facilities, equipment and other resources responsibly.

## 3. Affiliation and Governing Rules

The Club intends to be affiliated or registered with England Netball and, where applicable, its Regional and County Netball Associations and relevant leagues. The Club and its members shall comply with applicable England Netball rules, regulations, codes of conduct, safeguarding requirements, disciplinary and resolution procedures, and participation policies as amended from time to time.

## 4. Membership

Membership is open to persons who support the Club's objectives and meet applicable eligibility and registration requirements. Membership categories, subscriptions and reasonable eligibility criteria may be determined by the Management Committee. Members must comply with the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements.

## 5. Management Committee

The affairs of the Club are managed by a Management Committee accountable to the membership.

Core elected Officers are President / Chairperson, Secretary and Treasurer. Additional required governance roles include Safeguarding Officer, Data Protection Lead and Resolution Lead. The Committee may create, combine, divide, rename, fill, reassign or discontinue additional operational roles as required.

The names of current office holders are kept in a separate Committee & Role Register and do not form part of the Constitution.

## 5.4 Co-option and Vacancies

The Management Committee may co-opt an eligible Club member to fill a vacancy or additional Committee role between AGMs. A co-opted member serves until the next AGM unless otherwise determined under the Constitution. If a core Officer role becomes vacant, the Committee may appoint an eligible interim Officer until the next AGM or General Meeting at which an election can reasonably be held.

## 6. Committee Responsibilities

The Committee must act in the best interests of the Club and members, maintain appropriate governance, safeguarding, data protection, financial and risk arrangements, approve policies, maintain affiliations and records, manage conflicts of interest and report to members at the AGM.

## 7. Role Responsibilities

Detailed responsibilities and expected duties for each Committee role are maintained in separate role-description documents and reflected in the Club RACI matrix.

## 8. Safeguarding and Conflicts

The Club follows applicable England Netball safeguarding policies and procedures. The Safeguarding Officer is the normal Club contact. Where the Safeguarding Officer has a conflict of interest, is the subject of a concern or cannot act independently, the Resolution Lead acts as the alternative point of contact and ensures appropriate referral. If both are conflicted, the matter should be referred to the appropriate England Netball safeguarding contact or other competent authority.

## 9. Committee Meetings

The Committee should normally meet at least quarterly and may meet more often as required. Meetings may be in person, remote or hybrid. The quorum is three Committee members including at least one core Officer. Decisions are normally by simple majority, with a casting vote for the Chair where required. Urgent decisions may be made electronically if properly recorded.

## 10. Annual General Meeting

The Club holds an AGM once in each calendar year. Members should receive at least 14 days' notice. The AGM receives activity and financial reports, elects core Officers and considers properly notified motions. Members may attend electronically where identity and participation can reasonably be verified.

## 11. Extraordinary / Special General Meetings

The President / Chairperson or Management Committee may call an EGM when necessary. An EGM should also be called following an appropriate written request from members. Only notified business may be decided at the EGM.

## 12. Finance

Club funds must be used solely to further Club objectives. The Committee must maintain appropriate banking and financial controls. Detailed spending limits, approvals, payment controls and reporting requirements are defined in the Financial Management & Delegated Authority Policy.

## 13. Data Protection, Equality and Club Policies

The Club processes personal information lawfully and responsibly, promotes equality, diversity and inclusion and follows applicable England Netball participation and eligibility policies. The Committee may adopt and amend policies and procedures consistent with the Constitution.

## 14. Conflicts of Interest

Committee members must disclose actual or potential conflicts promptly. The remaining unconflicted Committee members decide how the conflict is managed, which may include leaving the discussion, withholding information or abstaining from a vote.

## 15. Amendments to the Constitution

The Constitution may be amended only by voting members at an AGM or EGM. Proposed amendments must be included with the meeting notice and require the specified member approval threshold.

## 16. Dissolution

The Club may be dissolved by the required member resolution. Remaining assets, after liabilities, must not be distributed to members and should transfer to another community netball, sports, charity or not-for-profit body with similar objectives.
