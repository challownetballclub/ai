# RACI Responsibility Matrix

Who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed across the main activities of the Club.

## How to read the RACI

R = Responsible — does or coordinates the work.
A = Accountable — owns the outcome or decision.
C = Consulted — provides input before the decision or work is completed.
I = Informed — kept aware of the outcome or progress.

## Key ownership

Governance / Constitution — President accountable, Secretary responsible.
Committee meetings — President accountable, Secretary responsible.
Budget / financial reporting — Treasurer accountable and responsible.
Membership / registrations — Registrar & Membership Officer accountable and responsible.
Safeguarding — Safeguarding Officer accountable and responsible; Resolution Lead is the alternative point of contact when the Safeguarding Officer is conflicted.
Data protection — Data Protection Lead accountable and responsible.
Complaints / disputes — Resolution Lead accountable and responsible.
Volunteer coordination — Welfare & Volunteer Coordinator accountable and responsible.
Coaching programme — Head Coach accountable and responsible.
Communications, events and sponsorship — Communications, Events & Sponsorship Officer accountable and responsible.
Equipment / facilities — Equipment & Facilities Manager accountable and responsible.
