# Conflict of Interest Policy

How committee members identify, declare and manage personal or financial conflicts.

## Purpose

This policy ensures Committee decisions are made in the interests of Challow Netball Club and are not improperly influenced by personal, family, financial or other interests.

## What is a conflict?

A conflict can include a direct financial interest, a benefit to a family member or connected person, a personal dispute that could affect impartiality, another loyalty that conflicts with Club interests, or any situation that could reasonably create the appearance that a decision is not independent.

## Declaration and management

Committee members must declare actual or potential conflicts as soon as they become aware of them. The Secretary should maintain a Conflict of Interest Register. Unconflicted Committee members decide how the conflict is managed, including exclusion from discussion, information or voting where appropriate.

## Quorum and voting

A conflicted person excluded from a decision should not count toward the quorum for that item where their participation is restricted. If too few unconflicted members remain, the matter should be deferred, referred to members or supported by independent advice as appropriate.

## Annual declaration

Committee members should review and confirm their standing interests annually and update them whenever circumstances change.
