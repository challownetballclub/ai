# Secretary

Core elected Officer. Current role holder: Katie. Typical commitment: 2–4 hrs/month normally; 5–8 hrs around AGM/EGM.

## Current role holder

Katie

## Role category

Core elected Officer

## Typical time commitment

2–4 hrs/month normally; 5–8 hrs around AGM/EGM

## Key responsibilities

• Coordinate Committee and General Meetings, notices and agendas.
• Take and circulate accurate minutes and maintain governance records.
• Maintain the current Constitution, policies, role register and key decision records.
• Manage formal Club correspondence.
• Support required notifications and administrative governance activity.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
