# Equipment & Facilities Manager

Operational role. Current role holder: Narelle. Typical commitment: 0.5–1.5 hrs/month; 3–5 hrs at season start/end.

## Current role holder

Narelle

## Role category

Operational role

## Typical time commitment

0.5–1.5 hrs/month; 3–5 hrs at season start/end

## Key responsibilities

• Maintain oversight of Club equipment, storage and property.
• Coordinate issue, return, replacement and stock checks.
• Coordinate facility requirements and access issues.
• Provide expected costs to the Treasurer before commitments.
• Maintain a basic inventory and flag safety concerns.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
