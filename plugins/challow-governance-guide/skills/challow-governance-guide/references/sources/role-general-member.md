# General Committee Member

Committee member. Current role holder: David / Rachel. Typical commitment: 1.5–3 hrs/month plus agreed project work.

## Current role holder

David / Rachel

## Role category

Committee member

## Typical time commitment

1.5–3 hrs/month plus agreed project work

## Key responsibilities

• Attend and contribute to Committee meetings.
• Review papers and participate in governance decisions.
• Take ownership of agreed projects or actions.
• Provide constructive challenge and support.
• Declare conflicts and act in the Club's interests.
• Support succession planning and volunteer recruitment.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
