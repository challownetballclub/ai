# Complaints & Resolution Policy

A simple local process for routine complaints and disputes, while routing safeguarding and disciplinary matters into the proper process.

## Purpose

This policy provides a simple local route for concerns about Club services, administration or interpersonal issues while ensuring safeguarding and disciplinary matters are referred into the correct England Netball process.

## Principles

Deal with concerns promptly, fairly and proportionately; use informal resolution where appropriate and safe; maintain confidentiality; manage conflicts; do not use this process to investigate safeguarding matters; and keep proportionate records of formal complaints and outcomes.

## Routes

Routine administration issue — raise with the relevant role holder.
Interpersonal or Club complaint — Resolution Lead.
Safeguarding concern — Safeguarding Officer / England Netball process.
Potential Code of Conduct breach — applicable disciplinary route.
Concern about Resolution Lead — President / Chair or another unconflicted Officer.
Concern about President / Chair — Resolution Lead and an unconflicted Committee member.

## Local complaint process

Stage 1: informal resolution where appropriate.
Stage 2: written complaint to the Resolution Lead if unresolved or unsuitable for informal handling. The Resolution Lead checks jurisdiction and conflicts, decides the appropriate route, and ensures a proportionate process and written outcome where appropriate.

## Safeguarding conflict

If Louise as Safeguarding Officer is conflicted or cannot act independently, Narelle as Resolution Lead becomes the alternative Club point of contact and ensures appropriate safeguarding referral. If both are conflicted, the matter should be referred directly to the appropriate England Netball safeguarding contact or other competent authority.
