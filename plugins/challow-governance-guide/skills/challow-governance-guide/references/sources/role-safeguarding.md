# Safeguarding Officer

Required governance role. Current role holder: Louise. Typical commitment: 1–2 hrs/month baseline; concerns may require additional time.

## Current role holder

Louise

## Role category

Required governance role

## Typical time commitment

1–2 hrs/month baseline; concerns may require additional time

## Key responsibilities

• Act as the Club's primary safeguarding point of contact.
• Promote compliance with England Netball safeguarding policies.
• Receive concerns and ensure appropriate recording, escalation or referral.
• Support safeguarding awareness and training.
• Maintain appropriate confidentiality.
• Use the Resolution Lead as alternative point of contact where conflicted.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
