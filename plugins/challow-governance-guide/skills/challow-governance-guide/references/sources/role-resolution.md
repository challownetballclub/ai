# Resolution Lead

Required governance role. Current role holder: Narelle. Typical commitment: 0.5–1 hr/month baseline; disputes may add several hours.

## Current role holder

Narelle

## Role category

Required governance role

## Typical time commitment

0.5–1 hr/month baseline; disputes may add several hours

## Key responsibilities

• Lead appropriate handling or referral of complaints and disputes.
• Encourage proportionate informal resolution where appropriate.
• Ensure formal matters use the correct Club or England Netball process.
• Maintain impartiality and declare conflicts.
• Act as alternative safeguarding contact when required.
• Keep proportionate confidential records.

## Expectations of all committee members

Attend and prepare for relevant meetings; complete agreed actions; maintain confidentiality where appropriate; declare conflicts of interest; follow the Constitution, Club policies and applicable England Netball requirements; and ask for support early if workload becomes unmanageable.
